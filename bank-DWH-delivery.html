ARCHITECT'S PROPOSAL · 28 SEPTEMBER 2026 · ON PREMISES

Deliver trusted reporting, one business process at a time

Two coordinated workstreams: reduce operational reporting load and establish reusable enterprise history. Repository notes inform this proposal; internal facts and service levels still require validation.

Operational serving

Sources → supported replication / ingestion → current-state read models → operational reports and application APIs.

A replica may be enough for unchanged queries. Add ODS integration only where it provides value. Transactional decisions need appropriate consistency.

Enterprise history

Sources → replayable ingestion → integrated atomic history → dimensional marts → MicroStrategy, APIs and controlled files.

Share identifiers, reference data and approved transformations across both paths. ODS is not a mandatory dependency for every EDW feed.

Delivery sequence and exit conditions

Indicative windows from mobilization, not contractual delivery dates. Access, staffing, procurement and acceptance govern the schedule.
